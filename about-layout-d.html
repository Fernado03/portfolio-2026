I take systems apart to learn how they decide — then rebuild them for people.

I’m a final-year Data Science student at Universiti Malaysia Sabah, based in Kuching. My focus is retrieval systems and computer vision: from a thesis reaching 71.6% F1 under noisy, missing inputs to internship work turning document archives into retrieval tools and CCTV footage into structured service records. Four hackathons brought a Gold, second place and two top-8 finishes. Off-hours, I fish.
