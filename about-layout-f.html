03 — About

A person before a profile.

Final-year Data Science student at Universiti Malaysia Sabah, based in Kuching.

I started out writing small game mods, which became a habit of taking systems apart to see how they decide things. Today that curiosity is focused on retrieval systems and computer vision. My thesis benchmarked multimodal emotion models under noise and missing inputs, reaching 71.6% F1. During my internship, I turned a client's document archive into a queryable assistant and CCTV footage into structured service records. I build the model, then the interface that puts it in someone's hands. Off-hours, I fish.

Four hackathons: one Gold, one second place and two top-8 finishes, spanning team leadership, backend ownership and solo full-stack delivery.
